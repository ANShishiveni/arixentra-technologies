# Arixentra Technologies — Website Brand Guide

**Brand promise:** Thoughtful software, connected infrastructure, and practical security.

**Tagline:** Innovate. Connect. Secure.

## Identity

- Use the approved abstract **A** symbol with a node/network motif.
- Preferred horizontal mark: `assets/arixentra-full-logo.png` (transparent PNG)
- Compact mark: `assets/arixentra-mark.png` or lightweight `.webp`
- Never recolor with unapproved blue/teal: the approved palette is navy, yellow, white and slate gray.
- Keep generous surrounding space. Do not use the fine tagline at favicon sizes.

## Colors

| Role | Color |
|---|---|
| Deep navy | `#0F172A` |
| Golden yellow | `#FACC15` |
| White | `#FFFFFF` |
| Slate gray | `#475569` |
| Website dark variation | `#0A1426` |
| Light surface | `#FAFAF8` |

Yellow is an accent, not a general long-paragraph text color. Dark navy on yellow and white on navy offer high-contrast combinations.

## Typography

- Headings: Manrope, weight 700–800, slightly tightened letter spacing.
- Body: Inter, weight 400–600, comfortable line height.
- Eyebrows and metadata: Inter, weight 800, uppercase and carefully spaced.

## Motion principles

- The website's rotating 3D node sphere is the primary decorative motion element.
- Animation is restrained, responsive and stopped when `prefers-reduced-motion` is active.
- No parallax or video loops are required elsewhere.

## Voice

- Professional without becoming impersonal.
- Direct, outcome-oriented and technically credible.
- Avoid unsupported claims about customer numbers, accreditations, awards or years of operation.
